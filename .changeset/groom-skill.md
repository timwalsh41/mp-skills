`groom` brings a raw ticket to Groomed in place: it grills the user to fill the groomed fields, then writes them and moves the ticket to the groomed status, reading the field names and transition from the repo's tracker doc. The counterpart to `/to-tickets` for tickets that already exist, filling the gap for tickets that arrive raw from triage or a wayfinder decision. The issue tracker templates now document a "When a skill says 'groom a ticket'" section so the contract is tracker-specific but the skill stays generic.
